$ shore excursion — included — Napoleon's villa, Portoferraio

Portoferraio has been catching people who didn't entirely choose it since 1814, and it caught us in the same spirit, a horseshoe of a harbour with the old town stacked up behind it in the standard Italian arrangement of laundry and geraniums, and above the lot of it the villa, because when a town has had exactly one famous resident it does not waste him.

The included excursion went up the hill, and the first thing the villa does is fail to be big enough, which is the whole exhibit really, the man had been running a continent the previous spring and here was a house you could walk end to end in the time it takes to lose a tour group, gardens included, with a terrace from which he could inspect his entire jurisdiction without binoculars.

We did the rooms at the pace the group allowed, which is the pace of the slowest photographer, and the rooms held their own, small windows, sea light, furniture built for a man planning to be elsewhere.
