The 13:31 out of St Pancras did what it always does, which is make France feel like a suburb, and at 16:49 we were at Gare du Nord with eighty kilos of luggage and seventy minutes to cross town for the 17:56 to Lyon.

At the métro barriers a man wearing a lanyard explained that he worked there, which he did not, and two colleagues of his arranged themselves behind us, and there followed a short conversation in two languages about whether our tickets and our bags required his assistance, which they did not. Claire was behind me the whole time. The tickets stayed where they were, the three gentlemen went off to find easier company, and we missed the 17:56 by one minute, which is the part I am still filing paperwork about.

The 19:00 took us to Lyon instead — a city neither of us had planned to sleep in, which is the kind of navigational error you would pay money for, and in a sense we had.
