We came off the ship at 08:00 into a city built by an order of knights with a great deal of money and a grudge, and the first thing Valletta does is go uphill, which after ten nights of lifts and buffets the party received as a provocation. The Knights put the place up in a hurry after nearly losing the whole island, and it shows, in that everything is a fortress, including a fair proportion of the churches, and the streets run straight down to the harbour so the sea can see exactly what it is dealing with.

We had been following Caravaggio around the Mediterranean like creditors, and Valletta is where the trail ends, because the Oratory of St John's holds the Beheading, the biggest thing he ever painted and the only one he ever signed.
