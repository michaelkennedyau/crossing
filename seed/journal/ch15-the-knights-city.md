Off the ship at 08:00. Valletta's first move is uphill. After ten nights of lifts and buffets, the party received this as a provocation.

The Knights built the place in a hurry after nearly losing the whole island, and it shows. Everything is a fortress, including a fair proportion of the churches. The streets run straight down to the harbour so the sea can see exactly what it is dealing with.

We had been following Caravaggio around the Mediterranean like creditors. Valletta is where the trail ends. The Oratory of St John's holds the Beheading — the biggest thing he ever painted, and the only one he ever signed.
