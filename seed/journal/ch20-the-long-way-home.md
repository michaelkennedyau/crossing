Nothing had gone wrong yet. I would like that noted for the file.

This one I wrote off the confirmation emails, on the third of September — the only chapter that's all paperwork. The paperwork said BA619 out of Palermo at 12:05, Heathrow at 14:20. Then six and a half hours of Terminal 3 before QF2 at 20:50, the bulk of it to be served in the First lounge, where conditions are historically superb.

$ BA619 PMO–LHR 12:05 → 14:20 · QF2 LHR–SYD dep 20:50 · Premium Flex

QF2 had been the Wednesday until it wasn't. That is what Premium Flex is for. You buy the fare knowing the plan will move, then you let it move.

One piece was still in the air: the bid for the front cabin, sitting in a Qantas machine somewhere, marked pending. Pending is where this chapter has to leave it.

An afternoon is a long tenancy in a lounge, even that one. I had it pencilled in as the trip's last stretch of proper luxury — six hours of other people's hospitality between us and the gate. None of what actually happened in there made it into any email.

The bookings ended the way bookings always do. Cleanly. No weather, no lanyards, no one-minute margins.

QF2 down in Sydney on the Saturday morning, the whole thing behind us. That is where the paperwork stops.
