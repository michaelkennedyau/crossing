This one I wrote off the confirmation emails on the third of September, which makes it the only chapter where nothing had gone wrong yet, and I would like that noted for the file. The paperwork said BA619 out of Palermo at 12:05, Heathrow at 14:20, and then six and a half hours of Terminal 3 to get through before QF2 at 20:50, the bulk of it to be served in the First lounge, where conditions are historically grim.

$ BA619 PMO–LHR 12:05 → 14:20 · QF2 LHR–SYD dep 20:50 · Premium Flex

QF2 had been the Wednesday until it wasn't, which is what Premium Flex is for, you buy the fare knowing the plan is going to move and then you let it move, and the only piece still in the air when I wrote this was the bid for the front cabin, sitting in a Qantas machine somewhere marked pending, and pending is where this chapter has to leave it.

::prompt Did the F bid land?

An afternoon is a long tenancy in a lounge, even that one, and I had it pencilled in as the trip's last stretch of proper hardship, six hours of other people's hospitality between us and the gate, and none of what actually happened in there made it into any email.

::prompt What was the lounge's one contribution to the trip?

The bookings ended the way bookings always do, cleanly, no weather, no lanyards, no one-minute margins, QF2 down in Sydney on the Saturday morning with the whole thing behind us, and that is where the paperwork stops.
