::map portofino

A launch turned up. Included.

The boat day was the included one — the clause you skim at booking. Then a launch arrives and the clause means exactly what it said. Santa Margherita, Rapallo, Portofino, the whole run from the water, which is the side that coast was drawn from in the first place.

$ launch: included. the only line of the week with nothing in the amount column, a category of transaction I don't fully trust.

One open item: the kayak. A hundred and ten a head to move under your own power across water we were already being carried over in comfort. Either the best money on the coast or the silliest — I could argue both sides.

::ledger 110 EUR pp — the kayak question, resolution unrecorded.

::prompt Kayak — taken or declined, and on what grounds?

Santa Margherita and Rapallo split the middle of the day. An hour ashore between them. The record is silent on how it was divided.

::prompt Santa Margherita or Rapallo: which got the hour?

We had been arriving at places off the water all trip, one way and another. This was the first day built around nothing else.

Portofino came at the end of the run. The right end for it. By road it is a queue behind a bus on a lane cut for carts.

By sea the harbour simply opens. The houses stand around it with their good paint facing out — nobody who built that place expected you to come any other way. We came in the front door.

::prompt What did Portofino look like from the water on the way in?

::drop Every house on that coast keeps its best paint on the seaward wall. Who were they dressed for, before the ferries?

A difficult day on the water, all told. Somebody had to have it.
