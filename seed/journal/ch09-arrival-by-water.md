The boat day was the included one, which is the sort of clause you skim at the booking stage and then a launch turns up and the clause means exactly what it said. Santa Margherita, Rapallo, Portofino, the whole run from the water, which is the side that coast was drawn from in the first place.

$ launch: included. the only line of the week with nothing in the amount column, a category of transaction I don't fully trust.

There was one open item on the day, the kayak, a hundred and ten a head to move under your own power across water we were already being carried over in comfort, which is either the best money on the coast or the silliest, and I could argue both sides.

Santa Margherita and Rapallo split the middle of the day, an hour ashore between them and the record silent on how it was divided.

::prompt Santa Margherita or Rapallo: which got the hour?

We had been arriving at places off the water all trip, one way and another, and this was the first day built around nothing else. Portofino came at the end of the run, which is the right end for it. By road it is a queue behind a bus on a lane cut for carts. By sea the harbour simply opens, and the houses stand around it with their good paint facing out, because nobody who built that place expected you to come any other way. We came in the front door.

::drop Every house on that coast keeps its best paint on the seaward wall. Who were they dressed for, before the ferries?

It was, all told, a difficult day on the water, and somebody had to have it.
