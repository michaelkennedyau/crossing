Two countries in one day. No airport involved.

The St John Paul II left Valletta at 07:30. Virtu are firm on the point that she is the largest high-speed catamaran in the Mediterranean. That is the sort of fact a boat leads with when it would rather you thought about size than movement.

An hour and three quarters of open water, most of it before nine. Pozzallo at 09:15. As ports go, less a destination than a thing ferries admit to on the way past.

$ Virtu Ferries, St John Paul II, 07:30 Valletta–Pozzallo, ref 10729224

The land side went to the trade channel. One car, one driver, 230 kilometres from the bottom-right corner of the island to the top-left. The quotes came back between €280 and €380 — a hundred euros of daylight on the same road, with the same amount of Sicily in the middle.

::prompt Which driver quote won, and how was it found?

::ledger 280-380 EUR — the driver, quote range, winner unrecorded

Whoever won carried us through the interior. The part the postcards skip. Wheat, distance, towns up on their hills watching the road go by without much interest.

By early afternoon, Villa Igiea. Started the day in one country, finished the drive in another, a sea in between. The correct way to do it, and almost never the way it gets done.
