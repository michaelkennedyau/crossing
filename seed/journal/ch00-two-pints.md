Twenty-six hours out of Saigon the seat turned left instead of right, which we took calmly, the way you take weather, and by six that evening we were at a table off Kensington High Street with two pints and no plan beyond the general direction of Europe.

The trip drew itself somewhere in the second pint, the way the good ones do — nobody proposed anything, the map just started talking, and by the time the glasses were empty there was a shape on the table that nineteen days later would turn out to have been roughly right about everything except the parts it was completely wrong about.
