::map lipari

Lipari is the big one, which in the Aeolians is a relative term, and the day began in the port because on Lipari everything does, the island being arranged around its harbour the way an office is arranged around the kettle.

The town runs between two harbours with a castle rock in the middle, which is where you put a castle when six thousand years of neighbours keep arriving by boat.

::prompt How did we get around the island — and what did it cost, agreed when?

::drop For about four thousand years the sharpest edge in the known world came off this island, and half the Neolithic Mediterranean carried a flake of Lipari black in its kit. Silicon Valley, same margins, better weather.

The north end is pumice country, whole hillsides quarried and shipped by the sack until UNESCO suggested they stop selling the scenery, and the water below the old workings sits over a white seabed that makes the colour the brochures are always lying about, except here.

::drop Pumice is the stone that floats. They dug up a mountain here light enough to swim home.

The afternoon belonged to the water, which was the standing arrangement on this leg and was not renegotiated.
