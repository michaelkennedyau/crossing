There's a castle on a rock in the middle of town. That's where you put one when six thousand years of neighbours keep arriving by boat.

Lipari is the big one — in the Aeolians, a relative term. The town runs between two harbours. The day began in the port. On Lipari everything does. The island is arranged around its harbour the way an office is arranged around the kettle.

::prompt How did we get around the island — and what did it cost, agreed when?

::drop For about four thousand years the sharpest edge in the known world came off this island, and half the Neolithic Mediterranean carried a flake of Lipari black in its kit. Silicon Valley, same margins, better weather.

The north end is pumice country. Whole hillsides quarried and shipped by the sack, until UNESCO suggested they stop selling the scenery.

Below the old workings the seabed is white. The water over it is the colour the brochures are always lying about. Except here.

::drop Pumice is the stone that floats. They dug up a mountain here light enough to swim home.
